# Observation sheet

Filled in by the **observer** during the session, on paper or a laptop. It backs up the automatic logs (stopwatch times, task outcomes), records what the logs can't see (hesitation, confusion, signs of sickness), and lists moments to ask about in the interview. One sheet per session.

**What counts:**
- **Hesitation:** a pause of about 3 s or more where the participant seems unsure what to do next. Count it, and note the time if it's long or repeated.
- **Error:** **ROVR**: a command that was misheard, misunderstood, rejected, or moved them the wrong way. **Manual**: walking into a wall, a wrong turn they had to undo, or overshooting the target.
- **Help:** anything the facilitator said beyond the scripted instruction.
