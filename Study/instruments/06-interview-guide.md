# Semi-structured interview guide

**When:** after the last questionnaire round (after the House), before the debrief. **Time:** 10–15 minutes. **Recording:** audio, covered by the consent form.

It covers the topics in §7.1.3 (comfort, sense of control, how people expressed themselves, moments of anxiety, satisfaction with how the system reacted). The ROVR questions also revisit the friction points from the Wizard of Oz study (§5.4), so §7.1.5 can check whether they still occur in the finished prototype.

**For the interviewer:**
- Ask the core questions of both groups in this order, then the ones for the participant's condition. Skip a question if it was already answered.
- Stay neutral. Don't explain or defend the system, and don't say which condition is "ours". If they ask, say: "We're interested in your experience either way."
- Use the follow-up prompts when an answer is short: "Can you tell me more about that?", "What happened then?", "What did you expect to happen?", "Can you give an example?"
- Bring the observation sheet. Ask about the incidents you noted ("In the maze, you stopped for a while after the second turn. What was going on?").

---

## Opening (read aloud)

"Thanks, that's all the tasks done. To finish, I'd like to hear about your experience in your own words. There are no right or wrong answers, and negative comments are just as useful to us as positive ones. This part is audio-recorded, as on your consent form. Shall we start?"

## Core questions (both conditions)

1. **Overall:** How would you describe moving around in VR this way, in a few words?
2. **Worlds:** Which of the three worlds was the easiest to move around in, and which was the hardest? Why?
   - Prompt: What made the maze / the house different from the plain?
3. **Control:** How much did you feel in control of where you were going? Were there moments you felt you'd lost control?
4. **Getting lost or anxious:** Were there moments you felt lost, unsure where to go, or uneasy? What happened, and how did you get out of it?
5. **Mistakes:** When something didn't go the way you wanted, what did you do to fix it? How easy was that?
6. **Comfort:** How did your body feel during the tasks: eyes, head, stomach, balance? Did any part make it worse, such as turning or moving fast?
7. **Speed:** Did you change your walking speed? Why, or why not?
8. **Minimap (maze):** Did you use the map in the corner of the view in the maze? How did it help, or not?
9. **Effort:** Which part took the most concentration?
10. **Satisfaction:** If you used VR regularly, would you want to move around this way? Why, or why not?

## ROVR (voice) condition only

11. **Phrasing:** How did you decide what to say? Did that change over the session?
    - Prompt: Did you use distances ("five meters"), objects ("go to the door"), or something else? Which worked better?
12. **Not understood:** Were there times the system misunderstood you or didn't respond? What did you do next: repeat, rephrase, give up?
13. **Out of view:** Did you ever want to go somewhere you couldn't see yet, like another room? What did you do?
14. **Stopping and correcting:** How did stopping or changing direction mid-movement work for you? Did it react fast enough?
15. **Waiting:** How did the delay between speaking and moving feel?
16. **Questions back:** When the system asked you something (for example "Which door?"), how did that feel?
17. **Without the controller:** How was it to have no controller in your hands? Was there anything you wanted to do but couldn't by voice?

## Manual (controller) condition only

11. **Controls:** How did the thumbstick movement and turning feel? Was anything awkward?
12. **Planning:** How did you plan your route, in the maze and in the house?
13. **Thinking aloud:** How was it to talk through what you were doing while moving? Did it change how you played?
14. **Voice:** If you could also have spoken commands, such as "go to the kitchen", would you have used them? Where?

## Closing

18. Is there anything else about the experience you'd like to tell us?
19. If you could change one thing about this way of moving, what would it be?

"Thank you. That's the end of the interview." *(Continue with the debrief.)*
