# Screening and demographics

**When:** online, before scheduling. **Who:** anyone interested in taking part. **Time:** about 5 minutes.

It checks the eligibility criteria and records background that could explain differences between the two groups, such as VR, controller and voice-assistant experience. The answers marked **(eligibility)** decide who can take part. The rest describe the sample in the paper.

Collect contact details (section 1) in a separate sheet from everything else. When someone is scheduled, link their two records with the participant code only.

---

## Form text

**ROVR study: sign-up and screening**

Thank you for your interest in our study. ROVR is a thesis project by Computer Science students at De La Salle University. We're comparing two ways of moving around in virtual reality (VR). This form checks whether you can take part and asks a little about your background. It takes about 5 minutes.

Your answers are confidential. They're used only to check eligibility and to describe the participants as a group. Your name is never linked to your results.

## Scoring and use

- **Eligible:** 18+ (Q5), DLSU undergraduate (Q6), has used VR (Q7), English 3 or higher (Q8), no language difficulty (Q9), no uncorrected vision problem (Q10), and none of the safety exclusions (Q11, Q12).
- **Not eligible:** send a short, neutral thank-you that doesn't say which answer excluded them. Delete their record once screening is finished.
- **Pilot participants** (PP1–PP4) use the same form and must meet the same criteria.
- **Comparing the groups:** report Q13, Q17–Q21 and Q23 per condition. Large differences between the groups, for example far more gamers in Manual, are worth mentioning as a limitation.
- **On the day:** before putting the headset on, ask again: "Are you feeling well today? Have you had any alcohol in the last 24 hours?" Reschedule anyone who feels unwell.

Check the safety questions (Q11, Q12) with your adviser and the ethics committee: they may want them worded differently or covered in the consent form instead.
